Why I’m doing this

Almost everyone in my generation knows someone who has struggled with their mental health, and most of us know many. What I’ve learned is that it looks different for everyone. There is no single story, and no single kind of help that works.

What too often holds people back is stigma, the fear of being judged for something that is nobody’s fault. That’s why I’m running the Stramilano Half Marathon on 21 March 2027 to raise money for Mental Health Europe, which campaigns across Europe for better care, for people’s rights, and for an end to mental health stigma.
