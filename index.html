Scarlett Runs for CHIVA

Raising money for children and young people growing up with HIV

Why I'm doing this

Princess Diana showed the world that compassion can change how people treat children and families living with HIV. I want to carry that spirit forward. I've taught ballet to children, and I know how much it matters for young people to feel safe, supported and included.

On 21 March 2027 I'm running the Stramilano Half Marathon in Milan to raise money for CHIVA, the Children's HIV Association, which supports children and young people with HIV and their families across the UK and Ireland.
