Why I’m doing this

I’ve always been inspired by Princess Diana, who helped break the stigma around HIV and AIDS when so few people would. Many children are born with HIV. They didn’t choose it and couldn’t have prevented it, yet they can face a very different kind of judgement from the one people assume.

Through teaching dance, I’ve seen how quickly children get treated as “different”. It’s rarely the children themselves. It’s the fear and misunderstanding of the adults around them, like parents who won’t allow playdates. With modern treatment, children growing up with HIV can live full, healthy lives. What holds them back is stigma.

That’s why I’m running the Stramilano Half Marathon on 21 March 2027 to raise money for CHIVA, the Children’s HIV Association, which supports these children and young people, and helps everyone around them understand.
